How referral rewards work

One-level referral program · no payment for signups alone.

  1. Invite a new memberThey enter your code during registration or use your referral link. The referral is recorded once and cannot be changed later.
  2. They generate valid ad revenueOnly net revenue confirmed by an approved ad provider can generate a commission. Demo clicks and test interstitials do not count.
  3. Your commission is calculatedYou earn 10% of eligible referred-member net revenue for 365 days after signup.
  4. Admin reviews and approvesCommission stays pending until fraud checks and provider reconciliation are completed. Approved amounts are added to your wallet.

Self-referrals, fake accounts, incentivized/automated traffic, chargebacks and invalid traffic are excluded. LinkNova may reverse unpaid rewards for provider adjustments or fraud.
